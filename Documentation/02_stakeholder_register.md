# 02. Stakeholder Register: Project HelpLocal

## Purpose
This register identifies the key stakeholders for the HelpLocal platform, mapping their project roles, core interests, and level of influence to determine communication strategies.

| Stakeholder | Role | Project Interest | Influence Level | Communication Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seniors / Requestors** | Primary End Users | High - Need accessible, reliable assistance for physical chores. | High - Product usability must cater completely to this segment. | Regular focus groups and simplified onboarding feedback loops. |
| **Volunteers** | Secondary End Users | High - Want an intuitive interface to find local tasks and track impact. | Medium - Crucial for system liquidity, but do not drive core features. | In-app surveys and community forum updates. |
| **Admin Team** | Operations Support | Medium - Require robust backend tools to vet users and manage disputes. | High - System security relies on their operational workflow. | Weekly operational alignment syncs. |
| **Development Team** | Technical Delivery | High - Need clear functional requirements to architect and code the platform. | Medium - Responsible for execution rather than strategic direction. | Daily Standups and Sprint Planning sessions. |
| **Local Councils** | Compliance & Sponsors | Medium - Interested in community welfare metrics and regulatory compliance. | Low - Do not manage day-to-day but can halt operations if laws are broken. | Monthly progress documentation and compliance audits. |
