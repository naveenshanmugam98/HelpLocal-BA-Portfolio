# 03. Requirements Elicitation Plan: Project HelpLocal

## 1. Objectives
The objective of the elicitation phase is to interact with stakeholders, navigate operational and accessibility constraints, and translate community safety needs into clear software requirements.

## 2. Elicitation Scope & Schedule
We will execute a structured, 2-week elicitation process across our user and technical ecosystem to ensure a balanced approach to usability, operations, and compliance.

| Targeted Stakeholders | Elicitation Technique | Rationale / Key Focus Area | Timeline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seniors & Caregivers** | 1-on-1 Interviews | Uncover physical accessibility limitations (font scaling, button contrast, navigation simplicity) without overwhelming the users. | Days 1 - 4 |
| **Local Volunteers** | Digital Surveys | Gather high-volume data on user motivations, maximum preferred travel radius (5km benchmark), and device usage patterns. | Days 3 - 7 |
| **Admin & Operations Team** | Interactive Workshop | Collaboratively map out the internal verification workflow, dispute management steps, and back-office UI needs. | Day 8 |
| **Technical Architects** | Interface Review | Evaluate 3rd party digital Identity Verification (IDV) APIs (e.g., Stripe Identity) to determine technical integration feasibility. | Days 9 - 10 |

## 3. Ground Rules & Methodology
* **Active Listening:** Document user pain points verbatim before proposing any technical UI features.
* **Traceability:** Every requirement uncovered during these sessions must map directly to a business goal in the RTM.
* **Sprint Zero Alignment:** The output of this plan will directly feed into our Sprint 1 Product Backlog Refinement.
